About Peak Power

What Is Peak Power?

Peak Power turns the biggest data center planning question, where to build and how to power it, into something you can explore. It combines site screening, power-plan simulation and operating scenarios in one place, so a team can compare options and understand the trade-offs behind each one. Today it is a working prototype: a screening and decision-support tool, not a quote or a guarantee.

  • Developers

    Screen counties and compare projects before spending on land, studies and interconnection requests.

  • Operators

    Test how a power plan behaves through stress: grid events, wind and solar lulls, equipment outages.

  • Infrastructure investors

    See what a plan costs, how clean it is and what it gives up, with the assumptions shown rather than hidden.

  • Planning teams

    Compare sites and power plans side by side on cost, carbon and reliability, and keep the reasoning in one place.

The problem

  • Fragmented data. Grid queues, water, hazard, fiber and policy information sit in different sources, formats and years.
  • Costly decisions. Land, interconnection and equipment commitments are hard to reverse.
  • Uncertain energy. When power arrives, and how much, drives when revenue can start.
  • Operating limits. Grid stress and variable renewables constrain what can run, and when.
  • Hard comparisons. Few tools put sites and power plans side by side on cost, carbon and reliability.

Our solution

One planning environment that brings three things together:

  • Data. County-level screening across nine comparison dimensions, with sources and gaps listed in the Appendix.
  • Simulation. An hour-by-hour model of the power plan over ten years, including stress events.
  • Decision support. Start-month, cost, carbon and reliability results, with the reasoning shown.
