Where these grades come from
  • Geography: county boundaries from US Census data (us-atlas), distances to 57 metro anchors, computed here.
  • Fiber: distance to the nearest colocation or network facility, from the CAIDA IXP dataset (built on PeeringDB), Oct 2026 release. Facility locations only, not fiber routes.
  • Carbon reference: the US grid averages about 384 kg CO₂e per MWh (Ember 2025). There is no industry standard cap, so the caps here are examples. Tenants usually set clean energy goals instead.
  • Policy: state actions found in public sources in Nov 2026. Moratorium tracker, ArentFox Schiff, Venable, NCSL. Verify before relying on any state.
  • Energy: the hourly model covers ERCOT only, from the case packet. Outside Texas the Overall grade uses the regional grid queue wait as a stand in, until hourly prices for those regions are added.
  • Water: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 baseline water stress, matched to counties here.
  • Time to power: LBNL Queued Up (data through 2025), by grid operator area.
  • Wind and solar cap: EIA Hourly Electric Grid Monitor, 2025 net generation by grid operator (via Catalyst Cooperative's PUDL).
  • Hazard: FEMA National Risk Index v1.20 (Dec 2025), expected building loss per dollar of buildings.

Alerts and actions

Data and model. Power costs, carbon, demand and its tiers, wind and solar profiles, prices, grid schedule, stress events and the seven resource offers come from the symposium packet (v6.0.1, V2: the battery may charge from the grid, up to 53 MW); builds start from 384 pre-simulated plans, each replayed through its official checker, and are re-run hour by hour in your browser with the checker's accounting. Revenue models, demand growth, weather years, contract delays, forecast events and the rented generator ($350/MWh, 0.74 t CO₂/MWh) are ours and labeled. The packet does not allow selling power back to the grid, and the battery never exports. Cost is the packet's decision cost (energy, equipment, contracts, $123.50/MWh to move work, $1,000/MWh for paused batch work), constant 2023 dollars, undiscounted. Dispatch follows simple rules, not an optimizer.
