Data and model. Power costs, carbon, demand and its tiers, wind and solar profiles, prices, grid schedule, stress events and the seven resource offers come from the contestant packet; builds start from 384 pre-simulated plans that passed its official checker and are re-run hour by hour in your browser with the checker's accounting. Revenue models, demand growth, weather years, contract delays, forecast events and the rented generator ($350/MWh, 0.74 t CO₂/MWh) are ours and labeled. The packet does not allow selling power back to the grid. Cost is the packet's decision cost (energy, equipment, contracts, $123.50/MWh to move work, $1,000/MWh for paused batch work), constant 2023 dollars, undiscounted. Dispatch follows simple rules, not an optimizer.
