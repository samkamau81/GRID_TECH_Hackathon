Peak Power: Investor Pitch and Judging Guide

A speaker-preparation guide built only from the team's own files (rubric, packet, README, docs, prototype code, packet outputs). Prepared 4 Nov 2026. The campus, offers and scenarios in the benchmark are fictional. Numbers were read from stored files; the live prototype and the checker were not re-run for this guide.

How to use it

  1. Read Sections 1–5 once to understand the story. The tags show what you can say as fact and what you must hedge.
  2. Rehearse the script (Section 6) out loud twice with a timer, then the Q&A (Section 7) with a teammate asking at random.
  3. Review the cheat sheet (Section 8) five minutes before presenting. The appendix lists sources and gaps.

What the rubric text itself tells you. (1) Judges score only what they see and hear in 5 minutes. (2) They judge you against the priorities and requirements you state in the demo, so state your customer's priorities in the first 20 seconds of the demo. (3) They score how the tool handles each dimension, not whether the numbers are optimal. (4) Five equal criteria: do not spend the whole demo on one.
