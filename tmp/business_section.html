How Peak Power could become a business

This section is a proposal, not evidence. Peak Power has no customers, no revenue, no signed partners and no market sizing today. Prices, customer counts and rates below are assumptions the team made to show how a business could work, so they can be tested. They live in one file (js/data/business-model.js) and every table and chart on this page is computed from it.

Unit economics (assumptions)

Lifetime value is annual price × gross margin ÷ annual churn. Payback is acquisition cost ÷ monthly gross profit. These ratios follow mechanically from the assumed inputs; they are targets to test in pilots, not results.

Illustrative annual revenue scenarios

Three adoption paths over three years. Customer counts are assumed paying accounts at the end of each year; revenue uses the average over the year. These are not forecasts.
